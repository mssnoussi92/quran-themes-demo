# The review (pointer)

Owner's request, 2026-11-17: every session must know what to do when he says « on commence la revue (dimanche) »,
« on fait le point » or anything like it. The review is one, for the whole Page: **its procedure is the skill
`weekly-review` of quran-reels**; read it first and follow it, whole:
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mssnoussi92/quran-reels/main/.claude/skills/weekly-review/SKILL.md`
(public: `curl -sS` works from any session; or the GitHub tools, `get_file_contents`). Attach quran-reels
(`add_repo`, owner `mssnoussi92`, repo `quran-reels`) when its tools are needed.

What matters most, in case that file cannot be read:

- **Now or Sunday?** Said on the day or with « maintenant »: do it now, with him, findings in the chat and each
  decision as a choice question. For a day to come: the scheduled task « Revue de la semaine (Quran Words Daily) »
  does it every Sunday at 09:23 Paris and writes an issue in quran-reels; what it must look at goes in
  `docs/insights.md` of quran-reels (« À la revue du <date> »). Unclear: ask (« Maintenant (Recommandé) » or
  « Dimanche à 09:23 »).
- **Where the numbers are**: the page « Statistiques » of quran-reels
  (https://mssnoussi92.github.io/quran-reels/catalog/stats.html, `insights.json` on its branch `state`), never a
  file of this repository. TikTok screenshots he sends: the skill `record-stats`.
- **What the theme side adds**: `docs/retention/calendar.md` (the first day without a video, three weeks ahead) and
  what is produced but not on the calendar.
- **No conclusion from noise**: under 3 videos or 12 points apart, « pas encore de différence ». The review proposes,
  he decides; nothing is published, merged or changed on `main` by it.
